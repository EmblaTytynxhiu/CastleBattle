Castle Battle 1v1 🏰⚔️

About the Game

Castle Battle is a 1v1 PvP multiplayer game where two players control their own castles and fight against each other using cannons and cannonballs. The goal is to destroy your opponent's castle and become the winner.

Game Features

1v1 Player vs Player (PvP) battles.

Two castles fighting against each other.

Shoot cannonballs at your opponent.

Destroy the enemy castle.

Competitive gameplay.

How to Play

Start the game.

Control your castle.

Aim and shoot cannonballs at your opponent.

Destroy the opponent's castle.

Be the last castle standing and win!
